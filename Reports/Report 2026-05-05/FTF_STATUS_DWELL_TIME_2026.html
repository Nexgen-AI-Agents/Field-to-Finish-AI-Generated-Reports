What is dwell time? Every order moves through stages like stops on an assembly line. Dwell time = how long an order sits still at each stop before moving forward. Short dwell = efficient. Long dwell = a bottleneck eating your time and revenue. The lifecycle bar below shows exactly where your time is disappearing in 2026.
